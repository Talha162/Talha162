## A little about me

I'm **Muhammad Talha**, a Flutter-focused mobile engineer based in Islamabad, Pakistan, currently working as a **Mobile App Development Specialist at AI Systems**.

I enjoy turning complex requirements into polished, maintainable applications. My experience spans **healthcare, fintech, food delivery, construction, and motorsport**, with work on apps published to the **Google Play Store and Apple App Store**.

I work across the product lifecycle, from architecture and state management to APIs, offline sync, testing, CI/CD, and app-store releases. I'm especially interested in **real-time experiences, AI-assisted features, and reliable mobile systems**.
